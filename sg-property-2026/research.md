## Report Outline

### 1. Executive Summary (1 short paragraph)
Singapore's residential market enters mid-2026 in a two-speed state. Private prices keep edging up — six straight quarters of gains, driven by suburban (OCR) launches — while the HDB resale market posted its first decline since 2019 as a wave of MOP supply arrives. Rental growth has cooled to the low single digits; yields remain in the 3–4% range.
