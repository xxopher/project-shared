## 2. Key Market Trends (Outline Summaries)

### Trend 1: Deceleration and the "Soft Landing" of HDB Prices
After a rapid post-pandemic run (including a 9.7% spike in 2024), HDB resale price growth slowed to **2.9% in 2025**, culminating in the **0.1% index decline in Q1 2026**. This shows that government cooling measures (tighter LTV limits at 75%) and the massive pipeline of **100,000 completed BTO flats (2021-2025)** have successfully stabilized the broader market.

### Trend 2: The Surge in "Million-Dollar" Resale Flats
Even as mainstream prices plateau, the luxury public housing tier is thriving. Buyers are paying massive premiums for well-located mature flats near transport hubs. A record-breaking **1,594 flats** sold for over S$1 million in 2025, and **412 transactions** occurred in Q1 2026 alone, indicating high cash-rich demand in premium estates.

### Trend 3: Divergence Between HDB and Private Housing Indices
In Q1 2026, HDB resale prices fell by **0.1%**, while private home prices rose by **0.9%** (led by non-landed OCR developments rising **2.2%**). This highlights a structural split: private property buyers are fueled by capital reserves and new launches, maintaining upward price pressure despite private transaction volumes slumping by nearly **40%**.

## 3. Interactive Report Structural Outline

The single-page interactive report should be organized as follows:

*   **Header Section:**
    *   Title: *Singapore Property Market 2026*
    *   Subtitle: *An Interactive Visual Analysis of Public & Private Housing Trends*
    *   Metadata: Last updated with Q1 2026 official datasets.
*   **Executive Summary:**
    *   A clean, multi-column narrative introducing the "soft landing" theme and index divergences.
*   **Interactive Stat Cards (KPI Dashboard):**
    *   4-6 card components displaying core numbers (e.g., Overall Median Price, HDB Index Change, Q1 Transaction Volume, Million-Dollar Count).
    *   Hover states and micro-animations showing percentage shifts or context on click.
*   **Visual Chart Section (3 Dynamic Charts):**
    *   *Chart 1 (Bar/Line Combo):* HDB Resale Index Trend (showing transition from 9.7% growth in 2024 to 2.9% in 2025 and -0.1% in Q1 2026).
    *   *Chart 2 (Comparison Bar Chart):* Price Premiums by Estate (comparing mature/expensive medians like Queenstown/Toa Payoh against affordable medians like Jurong West/Woodlands).
    *   *Chart 3 (Doughnut or Stacked Bar):* Public vs. Private Divergence in Q1 2026 (contrasting HDB's -0.1% drop, URA overall +0.9% gain, and OCR private +2.2% growth).
*   **Estate Reference Tool (Interactive Filter):**
    *   A simple dropdown to filter and view median prices for key HDB flat types (3-Room, 4-Room, 5-Room) across major towns based on the research dataset.
*   **Sources & Methodology:**
    *   Footnote section mapping every key metric to its official URA, HDB, or data.gov.sg source.
